## Preserved behavior and unresolved limitations

- **General failed replacement still ends the previous hunt.** Preflight preserves it for exhausted slots or an unavailable player. Once preparation begins, `StartContract` still clears the old hunt before model/spawn/capture succeeds. A transactional replacement would require simultaneous old/provisional ped ownership and retained portrait ownership; the current code has one `C` and one `ownedPed`. No equivalent Rockstar sequence was established for retaining that old portrait through the new producer. This broader redesign was deferred under the working-card constraint.
- **Single-frame unavailable texture status still hides the owned card.** `MaintainPortraitAndCard` at lines 523–531 assigns validity from each lookup and immediately applies visibility; the game-owned fallback can be closed by `UpdateCard`. Delaying that reaction changes protected consumer behavior and could expose a stale texture. No proven equivalent engine sequence was found, so it remains unchanged.
- **The 32-slot session limit remains.** Normal startup now stops before exhausting reuse damages the current hunt. Neither `NextPhotoSlot`, the session mask, cache type/names, download sentinels nor the producer ordering was changed. Restart is still required for new contracts after every slot has been inspected.
- **Owned-ped cleanup remains conservative.** Original handles remain tracked through delayed deletion; native pointer zeroing alone is not confirmation. Model/script/persistence ownership mismatches block deletion instead of risking a foreign entity. Such a mismatch can leave later startup blocked while the handle still exists. Same-model handle recycling cannot be conclusively distinguished by these SDK probes. The existing cleanup tests remain green.
- **AI tuning is retained.** Movement 1 is Defensive; advancing mission enemies often use 2. That is a potential pursuit tuning choice, not a proven logic inversion. Ability/range, relationship groups, the eight-second contact-loss grace, ten-second search, one-second missing-task grace, three-second recovery interval, and native task adoption are unchanged. Guard/scenario tasks were not substituted for roaming.
- **Payout and clerk flows are retained.** Tested floor/ceiling, 25-cent rounding, wanted multiplier and photo-time cutoff agree with the documented policy. The $25 floor intentionally also applies after the wanted multiplier. Decorative-cash timeout/disappearance still uses the single guarded credit path. No handoff/payment task or money native was changed.
- **No gameplay train/tram implementation.** The [design note](train-tram-missions.md) documents verified trolley/train natives, staged loading, scheduling, carriage/scenario placement, ownership/cleanup and `ContractDef` fit. It explicitly identifies unknown arguments and the lack of a verified numeric single-player train-pool limit.

A source comparison against `46395fa` verified exact function text (normalizing CRLF only) for 26 protected functions: capture, lookup/release/handle checks, slot rotation/binding bookkeeping, portrait/card maintenance, texture binding/refresh, drawing, card object creation/destruction/inspection/attachment/title/render target, all three handoff functions, payout computation/settlement/update and giver prompts. `contract_data.h` and `handoff_logic.h` also match the baseline. `DrawCardFace` remains the final draw within `UpdateCard`, which remains last in the gameplay frame. The generic wait's pause bookkeeping changed outside these functions; wall-clock timeout and predicate order are retained.

## In-game checklist

1. **Repeated cards and U timing:** From idle, press U and check portrait/front/back. Put away and press U during the outro, including just after the prop disappears. Expect exactly one new contract once the native task retires; both faces must identify the new target. Repeat normally. I must reopen the current card. Pause/fade or enter a handoff while an outro request is pending: it must not execute afterward.
2. **Clerks and interruptions:** Receive contracts at two station clerks, flip/zoom/put away, interrupt one handoff, then reopen with I. Pause during preparation and during a handoff; gameplay deadlines must remain frozen. If a target disappears during preparation/interaction, expect clean failure or TARGET LOST without stale blips. Startup/cleanup failure logs beside the ASI are useful if this occurs naturally.
3. **AI and weapons:** Encounter fists, knife and revolver targets. Check for inherited extra guns. Provoke once; break LOS for eight seconds, remain hidden through the ten-second search, then return to sight. The target should search the last seen location and re-engage. Lasso, hogtie, release and knock down an engaged target: no forced task or weapon restart during restraint/recovery. Pay particular attention to cowering/fleeing after the 32768 correction.
4. **Law and payment:** Kill and photograph a target; U must reopen its card and retain the reward. Collect with Take Payment, then verify a second collection cannot credit again. Also exercise interrupted payment and the two-minute uncollected-cash fallback. For the corpse-loss regression, photograph a target, allow the corpse to despawn, then become wanted before collecting: the wanted reduction should still apply, subject to the $25 floor.
5. **Trails and target loss:** Enter/leave the 12 m Eagle Eye range, end a contract while another tracked trail is visible, and confirm only the contract's registration retires. If an unfinished target disappears, expect TARGET LOST and cleared blips; a photographed contract must remain collectable after corpse loss.
6. **Session exhaustion:** In a fresh session, issue and inspect 32 contracts. The next U replacement must report PORTRAIT SLOTS FULL promptly, retain the 32nd hunt, and let I reopen its portrait. Finish/photograph/collect that contract if desired. Restart the game to issue more contracts. This is a capacity mitigation, not a slot-reuse fix.
