# Target AI audit

Audited the five human-target bridge functions in `script.cpp` and `target_ai_logic.h` against the owner's local build-1491.50 decompiled scripts on 2026-09-06. Baseline: `46395fa`. This work does not change portraits, card inspection, handoff, or payment. Script behavior is evidence for configuration and call order; it does not establish every undocumented engine flag's complete meaning.

## Corrections

1. **An inherited weapon inventory bypassed the chosen loadout.** `SetupHumanTarget` only added the chosen weapon; an archetype could already carry other guns or melee weapons. Selecting unarmed did not remove those weapons, and combat attribute 54 allowed a gun target to equip the best inherited weapon. Setup now calls `REMOVE_ALL_PED_WEAPONS(ped, true, true)` once, before assigning the one randomly selected loadout. No weapon is removed, granted, rerolled, or forcibly drawn during task recovery or native-combat adoption. Rockstar clears the inventory before building the ambusher's loadout in [ambush_inb_forest_attack.c:3267](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/ambush_inb_forest_attack.c:3267>).
2. **Flee bit 32768 was configured opposite to the cited armed-enemy transition.** The old comment said enabling it permits cowering/fleeing and therefore set it false. Flaco's men set combat attribute 5 and flee bits 32768/512 true during their armed setup, then disable attribute 5, enable attribute 17, and clear both flee bits immediately before `TASK_SMART_FLEE_PED`. Setup now enables 32768. See [rcm_gunslinger2_1.c:28310](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_gunslinger2_1.c:28310>) and the opposite transition at [33206](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_gunslinger2_1.c:33206>). The source justifies matching that combat configuration; it does not justify inventing a symbolic name for the bit or guaranteeing that it alone prevents fleeing.
3. **The hogtying transition was missing from the task-interruption guard.** A ped being hogtied need not already report fully hogtied, lassoed, ragdolled, or getting up. That gap allowed the policy to request combat, search, or wandering during the transition. `UpdateHumanTarget` now includes `IS_PED_BEING_HOGTIED` in `canAct`. Rockstar explicitly tests being-hogtied separately from fully hogtied in [rcm_bh_laramie_sleeping.c:2049](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_bh_laramie_sleeping.c:2049>) and separately treats it as an interrupted interaction at [5461](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_bh_laramie_sleeping.c:5461>).

## Tasks, perception, relationships, and tuning retained

- `TASK_COMBAT_PED(ped, player, 0, 0)` is used directly by the rancher bounty at [rcm_bounty_rancher1.c:36532](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_bounty_rancher1.c:36532>). Other encounters pass different combat flags; there is no reason to copy those flags into this encounter without their surrounding task setup.
- The ex-confederate bounty only submits combat when `IS_PED_IN_COMBAT` is false at [rcm_bounty_exconfed1.c:33093](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_bounty_exconfed1.c:33093>). Its task-status helper accepts status **0 or 1** at [33113](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/rcm_bounty_exconfed1.c:33113>), matching the mod. The mod additionally debounces absence for one second and rate-limits retries to three seconds. Those delays are mod policy, not copied Rockstar timings.
- `beat_bounty_transport.c` guards each `TASK_WANDER_IN_AREA` behind a one-time bit at [5595](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/beat_bounty_transport.c:5595>) and `5605`. The mod's setup/state-transition issue pattern is consistent with this approach. Slum ambushers instead use authored scenario points (`beat_slum_ambush.c:7882-7889`). Those fixed staging positions do not improve a general roaming target; no scenario/guard task was substituted.
- Native-combat adoption retains the engine's task and current weapon. First scripted engagement selects the stored weapon; task recovery does not draw it again. No clear-task native is added.
- The decision policy is unchanged: unknown players do not trigger combat merely by proximity; threats or a new native-combat transition are remembered while a target cannot act; remembered players reacquire with clear LOS within 45 m; engaged sight is retained to 55 m; eight seconds without contact leads to the last *seen* position; search ends after ten seconds; reacquisition wins over simultaneous search expiry. Lingering engine combat or proximity through a wall does not refresh contact. `PlayerProvoked` intentionally requires headtracking, range, and LOS for aiming/intimidation, while consumed player damage is a separate input.
- Relationship groups are encounter choices: `rcm_bounty_exconfed1.c:7223` assigns `REL_PLAYER_ENEMY`; the rancher uses a mission-created group at `rcm_bounty_rancher1.c:7342`. Unconditionally assigning an enemy group at spawn could remove this mod's initially non-hostile roaming phase. Existing group behavior is retained, with direct combat targeted at the player when appropriate. No global relationship table is modified.
- Ability **2** is Professional; range **1** is Medium; movement **1** is Defensive according to the local `CCombatData__Ability`, `CCombatData__Range`, and `CCombatData__Movement` enums. Advancing enemies often use movement **2**, for example `rcm_bounty_exconfed1.c:15420`. Movement is a reasonable in-game tuning candidate if pursuit still feels passive, but the different mission roles do not prove the current value is a logic bug. Ability/range/movement are retained.
- `SET_PED_CAN_BE_INCAPACITATED(false)` controls incapacitation; the old adjacent comment's broader claim that this alone prevents surrender is not established by the scripts. Likewise `SET_PED_KEEP_TASK(true)` does not by itself prove that no future engine flee/cower response can occur. The encounter's explicit combat policy and flags are separate. Neither call was changed.
