# Routine activities and Saint Denis transit evidence

Research date: 2026-09-06. The local Rockstar scripts cited below are from `C:\Users\caleb\Desktop\RDR2 Coding\RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master\1491.50`. They establish actual game call patterns; native-shim tests cannot establish animation, prop, pathfinding, streaming or corpse behavior in the running game.

The routine implementation uses physical walking and native area wandering. The earlier forced in-place smoking/drinking tasks have been removed after the owner observed awkward stops in the road. The game can choose ambient pauses while wandering; their occurrence and placement remain an in-game check. True poker, blackjack, five-finger fillet, theatre attendance, shop transactions, haircuts and ambient tram travel remain disabled. Card clues describe selected destinations; visiting a street outside a shop is not implemented shopping.

## Opening hours and actual availability

The current exterior destination windows are **authored visiting hours**, not claimed Rockstar business opening hours. Public street, yard and waterfront visits avoid pretending to use a closed shop. Do not add a true shop, barber, game or show destination merely by setting a guessed hour range. It also needs confirmed world/story availability, physical access, occupancy and an interaction that accepts this scripted ped. Construction whose state depends on story progress remains excluded until its location-specific gate is established.

Theatre availability is explicitly more than clock time. [theatre_ticket_taker.c:1299](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/theatre_ticket_taker.c:1299>) displays the no-show message when `func_110` is false. [That helper at 2726](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/RDR2 Coding/RDR3-Decompiled-Scripts-master/1491.50/theatre_ticket_taker.c:2726>) reads `Global_1394141.f_1279[index /*8*/].f_3`. The following helper at 2735 also gates the Valentine magic-lantern door using world state and show state. These are build-specific script globals, not a supported read-only venue API. A successful door or scenario query would still not establish that a performance is running.

Schedules must consider expected arrival before closing and windows spanning midnight; selection must be rechecked after travel, interruptions and large game-time jumps. Unavailable destinations need another currently suitable destination, without teleportation or claims on the card that the target must be present at an inaccessible venue.

Exact SDK: [natives.h:10472](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/Bounty Hunting Mod/rdr2 scripting environment/inc/natives.h:10472>) declares `void TASK::TASK_RIDE_TRAIN(Ped ped, Vehicle train, int scenarioPoint, Hash scenarioHash)`; [6349](<C:/Users/caleb/Desktop/Bounty Hunting Mod/rdr2 scripting environment/inc/natives.h:6349>) declares `BOOL PED::IS_PED_ON_SPECIFIC_VEHICLE(Ped ped, Vehicle vehicle)`. The ride argument is a scenario point, not a generic passenger seat number.

Before enabling transit, establish all of: an existing trolley and unoccupied carriage point; a physical approach and boarding method; confirmation of the correct vehicle; safe scheduled disembarkation without changing the ambient route; bounded walking fallback; loss/streaming handling; combat/restraint exits; and a reachable corpse that is not deleted when Rockstar retires the borrowed vehicle. No verified combination was established here. Consequently the routine does not reserve, spawn, seize, stop, redirect, attach to or delete trains. Targets walk between destinations and the cards do not claim tram habits.
